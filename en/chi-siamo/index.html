Who Are We?

We are here to break down every barrier and prejudice that society has created towards people with DSA

DSA SMART START is a team of highly qualified language professionals, supported by psychology experts.

Since 2008 our team has supported young people and adults with dyslexia in learning English.

For over a decade, our team has been deeply committed to supporting young people and adults who experience difficulties in learning English.

OUR VISION

Our Mission

Our vision is founded on the belief that every individual deserves to be understood, supported and guided towards success, without being limited by labels or judgements.

We believe we can change how society perceives DSA. We are committed to providing practical tools for learning English and a positive environment for those who have yet to find the right support to achieve their personal goals.
